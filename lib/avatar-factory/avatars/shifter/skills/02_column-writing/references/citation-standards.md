# Reference: Citation Standards

## How to Cite Correctly in Shifter's Format

Shifter uses a hybrid citation system: inline citations for direct quotes and critical data, endnotes for background and supporting evidence. This preserves readability while maintaining accountability.

---

### Inline Citations

**Use for**:
- Direct quotations
- Specific statistics or data points
- Contested claims that require immediate authority
- Legal or regulatory references

**Format**:
> "Quote text" (Author/Source, Date)

**Examples**:
> "The inflation target remains non-negotiable," said Central Bank President Rosanna Costa (Bloomberg, June 3, 2026).
> Chile's unemployment rate fell to 7.8% in May (INE, June 2026).

**Rules**:
- Inline citation must not interrupt sentence grammar
- Place at the end of the clause or sentence
- If quote exceeds 25 words, set as blockquote with attribution below

---

### Endnotes

**Use for**:
- Background context
- Supporting studies or reports
- Alternative sources that confirm but are not primary
- Methodological notes

**Format**:
Use superscript numbers in text. List endnotes at column bottom.

**Example text**:
> The copper price surge has affected multiple sectors.¹

**Example endnote**:
> ¹ See London Metal Exchange, "Copper Cash Official Price," daily data, June 2026. Also analyzed in Goldman Sachs Commodities Research, "Copper Outlook H2 2026," May 2026.

**Rules**:
- Number sequentially from 1
- Place endnote section under heading `---` followed by `**Notes**`
- Full URL included for web sources
- Page numbers included for print/PDF sources when available

| Abbreviation | Full Name |
|--------------|-----------|
| INE | Instituto Nacional de Estadísticas (Chile) |
| BCCh | Banco Central de Chile |

| SEC | Superintendencia de Electricidad y Combustibles |

| LME | London Metal Exchange |
| IMF | International Monetary Fund |
| OECD | Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development |
| ECLAC | Economic Commission for Latin America and the Caribbean |

All other sources must be named in full on first mention.

---

### Citation Prohibitions

**Never**:
- Cite Wikipedia as a primary source (may use for orientation only, never publish)
- Cite without accessing the original (no "as cited in" chains)
- Cite a paywalled source without noting access limitations
- Fabricate or approximate a citation
- Cite social media without archived link and screenshot

---

### Verification Checklist

Before finalizing citations:
- [ ] Every inline citation has a corresponding entry in source registry
- [ ] Every endnote number has a matching note
- [ ] Dates are specific (month+year minimum; day+month+year preferred)
- [ ] URLs are tested and accessible
- [ ] No citation appears more than twice (diversify sources)
- [ ] Quotations are verbatim (no silent edits)
